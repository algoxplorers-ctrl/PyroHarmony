🧭 4-Zone Command Center Manual & Prototype Interpretation Guide

System: PyroHarmony v2.0.0 · Team: AlgoXplorers (Argentina) · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

ZONE 1 · Top Mission HUD, Telemetry & Bilingual Engine (EN / ES)

The top navigation HUD consolidates global mission metrics in real time: active biome of interest (AOI), Total Harmonized Fire Radiative Power (MW), active orbital satellites based on selected historical year, continuous 60 FPS WebGL telemetry, and the #btn-toggle-lang control that starts in English (EN) by default and instantly switches all UI components to Spanish (ES) with zero page reload.

ZONE 2 · 3D Sun-Synchronous Globe & True Nadir Optical Swath Cones

Renders planet Earth with NASA Blue Marble textures and atmospheric Rayleigh scattering, alongside true sun-synchronous polar orbits at 98.2° inclination for Terra (10:30 AM equator crossing), Aqua (1:30 PM), Suomi-NPP (1:30 PM), and NOAA-20 (+50 min). Contrasts real optical swath widths (2,330 km for MODIS vs. 3,040 km for VIIRS) and renders 0.1° × 0.1° thermal clusters using InstancedMesh with automatic GPU context recovery (webglcontextlost).

ZONE 3 · Control Panel, Z-Score Gauge & Tactical LACES Radar

Integrates the Semicircular SVG Thermal Anomaly Gauge (with ticks at -2σ, 0μ, +1σ, +2σ, +3σ), the Sensor Discrepancy Card (proving how a 3× to 5× raw hotspot count jump resolves into consistent Megawatts under α + β = 1.00), the Firefighter Tactical Radar (LACES Protocol) based on the 50-minute orbital gap between Suomi-NPP and NOAA-20, and instant export buttons for SitRep (.txt), GeoJSON (.geojson), and CSV (.csv).

ZONE 4 · Multidecadal Scrubber (2000–2026) & Burning Activity Calendar

The multidecadal timeline slider (with Year: and Week: controls) dynamically activates orbital satellites based on their official launch years (2000 Terra, 2002 Aqua, 2012 Suomi-NPP, 2017 NOAA-20). Below, an interactive 52 weeks × 27 years matrix (1,404 cells) displays the weekly climatological Z-Score heatmap with dynamic translatable tooltips.

3-Stage Evaluation Protocol

STAGE 1 Orbital Fleet Evolution (2000 ➔ 2026): Drag the year slider from 2000 to 2026 to observe the sequential addition of Terra, Aqua, Suomi-NPP, and NOAA-20, and the automatic switch between the Retrospective Proxy (2000–2011) and Dual-Sensor Harmonization (2012–2026).

STAGE 2 Ground Truth Benchmark Audit (Pantanal 2020, Week 35): Click the Pantanal 2020 Benchmark (W35) button in the HUD to verify that the gauge hits exactly Z = +2.82 (p < 0.005) with critical LACES alert.

STAGE 3 Open Science (FAIR) & Bilingual Testing: Toggle the language between EN and ES and export tactical SitRep reports and GeoJSON layers to confirm open data compliance.
